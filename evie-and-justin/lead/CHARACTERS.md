# Characters

Reference art: `../assets/characters/evie-and-justin-reference.png`

## Evie (white kitten)
- **Look:** fluffy white fur, black patch on her top-left ear/head, big **blue** eyes, pink collar with gold paw-print tag.
- **Personality:** the dreamer. Wonders out loud, notices small beautiful details, brave once she gets going.
- **Role:** asks "what if…?" and gives the emotional hook.
- **Speech:** soft, bright, lots of "ooh!" and "look!".

## Justin (black kitten)
- **Look:** dark fluffy fur, white-pink inner ears, big **green** eyes, dark collar with gold paw-print tag.
- **Personality:** the explorer/skeptic. Bold, funny, a little dramatic, secretly loves facts.
- **Role:** asks "but how does that work?" and drives the concept explanation.
- **Speech:** cheeky, confident, occasional mock-grumbles.

## Dynamic
Best friends who bounce off each other. Evie feels, Justin tests. They always solve the puzzle together.
Matching paw tags = their "adventure badge"; the tags can glow at the moment of discovery.

## Visual style
Pixar-style 3D, warm golden-hour lighting, soft fur shading, expressive big eyes. Keep collar colours and eye colours consistent in every shot.

# Lambs (introduced end of Ep.4, join the team from Ep.5)
Reference art: `../assets/characters/lambs-reference.png`
**Names are working names (English): Cotton & Toffee. Change freely.**

## Cotton (cream lamb)
- **Look:** curly cream wool, floppy pink-inner ears, big **blue** eyes, light-blue collar, gold bell with a heart cut-out, dark hooves.
- **Personality:** gentle, a little shy, a natural peacemaker. Knows the countryside because she lives there.
- **Role:** local guide; the "feelings" of the group alongside Evie. Her bell ring is her signature sound.

## Toffee (brown lamb)
- **Look:** curly chestnut-brown wool, cream chest and legs, brown floppy ears, big **green** eyes, green collar, gold heart bell, dark hooves.
- **Personality:** bouncy, silly, fearless but clumsy. Loves racing and jumping.
- **Role:** comic energy; gets into trouble, which creates the adventure.

## Dynamic with the cats
Cats = curious city-explorers who ask questions. Lambs = locals who know the land but not *why* it works. All four learn together. Cotton ≈ Evie (soft), Toffee ≈ Justin (bold), so pairs can split up and rejoin.
