# Lead decisions log (Tommie-approved)

## 2026-10-03
1. **Style:** stylised 3D cartoon, simpler fur (not realistic/Pixar-fur). Models made by Tommie (Meshy → Blender).
2. **Colours:** cleaner and brighter than the reference (Justin plum-black #352B3D, brighter Toffee chestnut, more saturated collars/eyes).
3. **LORE — why the kittens never age:** in Episode 1 the cats find a *special drink* and, because of it, they don't age. This is the origin of the series (explains why they stay kittens and keep going on adventures). Details (name/look of drink, where found, side effects, whether the lambs ever get it) are TBD — scriptwriters propose options.
4. **Kid-safety:** keep the 1953 flood in Ep.6 as a gentle passage but **remove the death toll / numbers of victims**. Children must be able to watch.
5. **Release rhythm:** 4-day rotation: **Day 1-3 = the 3 shorts of the episode, Day 4 = the full episode**, then rotate to the next episode (7 episodes = 28 days).
6. Defaults accepted (recommended options): unnamed off-screen guide in Ep.7; bell plant in Ep.2-3, clear in Ep.4; Ep.7 ends "more adventures soon"; lambs shown only in the Ep.5 cliffhanger short; wool = rounded clumps; EEVEE toon look; rig tier T1 for Ep.1; per-episode palette approved; logo = badge + wordmark.
7. Facts: spot-check unverified rows on open network before production.

## 2026-10-03 (round 2)
8. **The drink = Moondew Blossom** (pale-lilac flower cup holding a glowing droplet with a gold star; no bottle). Replaces "Moonmilk" in the Ep.1 script. Design: `design/props/SPECIAL_DRINK.md` option A.
9. **Origin:** nobody left it. The cats found it themselves on their adventure (Ep.1, Afsluitdijk area). No mystery giver, no tie to the bell, lambs or narrator.
10. **Paw-tag safety signal (new series mechanic):** the cats' gold paw symbol **turns GREEN when something is safe and RED when it is unsafe.** Used in Ep.1 to show the drink is safe for them, and as a recurring device in later episodes (e.g., tide/mud/heights). Accessibility: never colour alone, pair with a distinct sound + icon/shape cue (colour-blind friendly). Because real kids' tags don't exist, Ep.1 keeps a plain line that real children never taste things they find and ask a grown-up (recommended safeguard; Tommie can veto).
11. **Lambs and all animals:** eventually all animals get the drink, so nobody has to "grow up" on screen. They may have *some kind of birthdays* (celebrations without ageing). **In the LAST episode (Ep.7) the cats give the drink to the lambs.** Ep.1-6: cats only. Plant a gentle foreshadow (e.g., Cotton/Toffee mention wanting to stay little / birthdays) in Ep.5-6.

## 2026-10-03 (round 3)
12. **Second Blossom for the lambs (Ep.7):** the lambs are not in every episode, so the **cats** supply it: they either find another one or saved one earlier and share it. Lambs play no part in sourcing it. Scripts pick the cleanest option; if "saved", plant it lightly in Ep.1 and mention it once mid-season.
13. **Lamb consent framing approved:** each lamb says yes aloud, cats say it's their choice, tiny sip, tags green first, plus the "only works in stories / ask a grown-up" line.
14. **Paw signal wording:** show and explain it **fully once in Ep.1** (green = safe with chime + check-mark, red = unsafe with hum + X). After that, just *mention the paw whenever something happens and it changes colour* (short, natural lines like "Justin, your paw's red!"), no repeated full explanation. The non-colour cues (sound + symbol) stay on screen/audio for accessibility.
