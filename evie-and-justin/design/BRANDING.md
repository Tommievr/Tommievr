# Branding proposals (proposed v1.1 — awaiting lead approval)
Channel: **Evie & Justin's Little Adventures**. Style note v1.1: the flat, simple, bold cartoon characters in these mockups are close to the new stylised direction (no fur detail). All SVGs in `branding/` are **layout/colour mockups made from simple shapes** (generated by `branding/build_svgs.py`), not final art: the final logo should be redrawn from the approved character sheets (or from the first good Meshy renders). Open the SVGs in a browser. The palette board is `branding/palette-board.svg`.
Platform sizes below are from memory of YouTube's current guidance: **verify before export** (they change).

## 1. Brand basics
| Item | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Feeling | a bedtime storybook that woke up and went exploring: warm, cosy, curious |
| Colours | cream `#FFF1D6` (background), indigo `#2B2D52` (text), tag gold `#EBA02B` (badge/accent), sky `#8EC8F0`, peach `#FFD9A8`; Evie raspberry `#B3203C` and Justin green `#3C8A1C` as small accents. Each episode swaps in its accent (STYLE_GUIDE 2.2). |
| Fonts (free, Google Fonts, OFL) | **Fredoka** (display, rounded, friendly) for titles and logo; **Nunito** for body/subtitles. Fallbacks in the SVGs: Baloo 2, Trebuchet MS. Convert text to outlines for the final logo. |
| Signature device | the **gold paw tag** = adventure badge. It appears in the logo, lower third, episode number badge, and end card, and it glows at discovery moments. |
| Voice | "Goodnight" is always the last word of every video (Bible rule 5). |

## 2. Channel logo (avatar + wordmark)
**Avatar (`branding/logo-badge.svg`, 800×800):** a round badge, gold ring, inside: golden-hour sky over a green dike with a thin teal water line; **Evie and Justin's heads side by side** (white with patch and blue eyes, black with green eyes), collars and gold paw tags below. Must read at **98 px** (YouTube shows a circle): so only heads, eyes and the tags; no text in the avatar.
**Wordmark (`branding/logo-wordmark.svg`, 1800×600):** badge left + two-line type: **"Evie & Justin's"** (Fredoka Bold, indigo) over **"Little Adventures"** (Fredoka SemiBold, ~62 % size, gold), gold underline. Dark and light variants (light = cream text on indigo).
Ampersand idea (final art): the "&" drawn as two curled tails, optional.
Clear space = height of the "E". Minimum wordmark width 240 px.

## 3. Channel banner (`branding/banner.svg`, 2560×1440)
- YouTube safe area for all devices: **1546×423 px centred** (x 507-2053, y 508-931). Everything essential (wordmark + tagline) sits inside; the sides (TV/desktop only) carry the scene and the kittens.
- Layout: golden-hour sky with sun disc right of centre, rolling green ground, a teal water line; wordmark centred in the safe area, tagline below ("A tiny adventure to a big place, every week"); kittens left and right of the safe area (cropped away on mobile, which is fine).
- Final art: replace with a rendered **Blender wide shot** (`wide_two`, 16:9 upscaled) once Evie/Justin models exist. Add a faint silhouette of all 7 landmarks along the horizon (dike, church, bridge, hunebed, pine, pier row, bird) as a "season map".
- Add upload-schedule line only after the schedule is decided.

## 4. Thumbnail template (`branding/thumbnail-template.svg`, 1280×720, < 2 MB)
| Zone | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | the episode's **golden-hour background**, cats-brightest rule, accent-colour **rounded border** (16 px, radius 26) |
| Characters | Evie + Justin, `medium_two` or `close_up`, **big readable faces** with `surprised`/`curious` expressions, on the lower left/centre (rule of thirds); paw-tag rim glow on |
| Title | cream rounded box, upper left: **max 3 words**, 2 lines, Fredoka Bold ~ 80 px, indigo. Examples: "THE LAND BELOW THE SEA" (too long) → **"BELOW THE SEA?"**. Always a question or a mystery. |
| Episode badge | gold circle (the tag), upper right: **EP.n** |
| Keep clear | bottom-right **160×70 px** for the video-length badge |
| Look | high contrast, saturated characters, calmer background; test at 168×94 px (smallest suggestion size) |
| Shorts | reuse with 1080×1920 and a hook question; no text smaller than 70 px |
Series consistency: same border, same title box, same badge position in every thumbnail; only the accent colour and background change.

## 5. End card (`branding/end-card.svg`, 1920×1080, last 10-20 s)
- **Dusk look** (always): indigo → violet → rose gradient, calm goodnight beat.
- Text: "Goodnight, little adventurers" (headline) + "More adventures are waiting…".
- Slots (dashed boxes): **two video slots** 760×428 at left/right, **subscribe circle** bottom centre. Leave these **free of art** (YouTube places end-screen elements there; the layout must be repeated in the editor).
- Kittens `sleepy` expression bottom corners. Optional: bell sound sting for Ep.4+ (lambs).
- Duration: 10-15 s; the audio is the bedtime music + a soft "goodnight" (voice).

## 6. Lower third (`branding/lower-third.svg`, 1920×1080, transparent PNG/alpha)
- Position: x 120, y 800-930 (inside title safe, above the subtitle band at y 980+).
- Cream rounded bar 760×130, left accent bar in **the episode accent colour**, gold tag icon, line 1 **LOCATION NAME** (Fredoka Bold 48), line 2 "Province • one-line fact" (Nunito/Fredoka Medium 32, plum).
- Animation: slides in from left over 0.4 s ease-out, holds 4 s, fades 0.3 s. Use for location names and for "new word" cards ("Polder", "Sluice"): same bar, tag icon swaps for a small book icon.
- Facts on the bar must be on the verified list (Bible rule 1).

## 7. Episode title card (`branding/title-card.svg`, 1920×1080, 3 s)
- Background: the episode location, golden hour, no text baked in; sun disc; characters centred low in `medium_two`.
- Text: small caps "EPISODE n" with letter-spacing 8 (Fredoka SemiBold 54), then the **title in two lines** (Fredoka Bold 150, indigo, cream outline if the sky is dark), footer: the channel name (Fredoka SemiBold 40).
- Motion: gentle push-in on the background, text settles with a tiny bounce; ends on a warm cross-fade to the cold open (the title card comes **after** the 0:30 cold open).
- Shorts variant: 1080×1920, title in the centre column, y 300-700, safe from the UI zones (STYLE_GUIDE 4.1).

## 8. Extras (cheap, high value)
- **Watermark**: the gold tag, 150×150 px, 70 % opacity, bottom-right (YouTube branding watermark).
- **Playlist covers**: one per episode with the accent colour border.
- **Bumper (3 s)** for shorts: the tag glows, "Evie & Justin's Little Adventures" appears.
