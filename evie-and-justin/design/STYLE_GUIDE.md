# Style Guide (v0.1 — draft, not locked)

**Direction:** cartoony 3D-storybook. Keep the kittens' current soft-fur Pixar look; make worlds *simpler and more painterly* than the cats so the cats always pop.

- **Backgrounds:** soft shapes, slightly simplified textures, gentle depth blur. Dutch sky = big, with fluffy clouds (the Netherlands is famous for skies).
- **Palette:** warm golden-hour base; each episode gets one accent (Afsluitdijk = sea teal, Schokland = field gold, Giethoorn = canal green, Hunebedden = stone grey-purple, Kootwijkerzand = sand orange/heather purple, Delta Works = steel blue, Wadden = silver-pink mud flats).
- **Contrast rule:** cats are the brightest/most detailed thing in frame.
- **Consistency lock:** Evie = white, black patch top-left of head, BLUE eyes, PINK collar. Justin = black, GREEN eyes, DARK collar. Both: gold paw tag.
- **Scale:** world slightly oversized so kittens feel small and adventurous.
- **No** photoreal backgrounds, no text baked into images, no real people/brands.

## Per-episode background briefs
| Ep | Location | Must-have elements |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Afsluitdijk | Long dike road, sea on one side, lake on the other, tiny lighthouse/monument tower, huge sky |
| 2 | Schokland | Low grassy mound island in flat green fields, old church, old shoreline markers |
| 3 | Giethoorn | Narrow canals, thatched-roof houses, tiny arched bridges, whisper boats |
| 4 | Hunebedden | Giant stone table-tombs in heathland/forest clearing, mossy, mysterious but friendly |
| 5 | Kootwijkerzand | Rolling sand dunes, scattered pine trees, purple heather, wind swirls |
| 6 | Delta Works | Giant sea gates, calm water, big blue sky, dramatic but safe |
| 7 | Wadden Sea | Endless wet sand mirroring the sky, tidal channels, seagulls, distant sea line |

(Descriptions are visual direction only; real-world facts are checked by the script writers.)
