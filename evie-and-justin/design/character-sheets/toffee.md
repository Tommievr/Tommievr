# Toffee — character sheet (proposed v1.0)
Working name (see `lead/CHARACTERS.md`). Debuts end of Ep.4, joins from Ep.5. Reference: `assets/characters/lambs-reference.png` (right lamb). Shared specs: `COMMON.md`.

**Role/personality in one line:** bouncy, silly, fearless but clumsy; loves racing and jumping; gets into trouble.
**Acting notes:** big bouncy hops, overshooting stops, wobbly landings, head leads the body; faster timing than Cotton.

## Silhouette
Cloud-like scalloped body like Cotton, but with **darker mass, bouncier stance, ears a little higher**. Value contrast: the **cream socks and chest blaze** stand out against the brown wool. Cotton vs Toffee silhouettes must differ in tone (light vs dark), not just in the collar.

## Collar / bell
- Collar: thin green band, small stitch dots; green must read as **leaf green**, not olive-black.
- Bell: same gold heart bell as Cotton, separate object, swings harder (more bounce).
