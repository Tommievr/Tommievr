# Ep.4: Hunebedden, Drenthe — "The Sleeping Giants"
> **v1.1 style update (lead direction):** backgrounds are **simple, bold, clean 3D cartoon**: big flat colour shapes, 3-5 hero props, chunky bevelled forms, no texture noise, no normal-map grit, no fine detail. Facts/sources below are unchanged. Copy-paste prompts: `prompt-packs/04-hunebedden.md`.

**Accent:** stone grey-purple `#8B8499`. **Hero silhouette:** huge flat capstones on upright stones, a "table" in a clearing.
**Mood:** mysterious but friendly, soft mist. The episode ends on a cliffhanger: **a bell rings somewhere in the heath, two shadows** (lambs; see `lead/episodes/EPISODE_SLATE.md`): the set must have a **heath to the back** where the shadows can appear.

## What is true (and what we keep)
| Real element | Cartoon version |
|---|---|
| Hunebed D27 at Borger is the largest in the Netherlands, ~22.6 m long, 9 capstones on 26-28 uprights, with an entrance portal; built around 3400 BC, ~5,400 years old ([Stetson Univ.](https://www2.stetson.edu/neolithic-studies/passage-tombs/d27-passage-tomb-borger-drenthe-province-netherlands/), [Hunebed Nieuwscafe](https://www.hunebednieuwscafe.nl/2017/11/the-largest-hunebed-the-netherlands-d27-borger/)) | a long low table of stone: 5-7 capstones (simplified count), uprights beneath, a visible entrance gap. **Stylised, not a replica of D27.** |
| D27 sits in a small patch of woodland south of the Hunebedcentrum, shade of trees ([Modern Antiquarian](https://www.themodernantiquarian.com/site/9834/d27-borger)) | **forest clearing with oak/birch**, soft dappled light |
| Largest capstone about 20,000 kg ([Stetson](https://www2.stetson.edu/neolithic-studies/passage-tombs/d27-passage-tomb-borger-drenthe-province-netherlands/)) | the capstones are big and rounded, **mossy on top**, rust-lichen speckles `#C98B4B` |
| Stones are glacial boulders (not quarried): rounded, irregular grey granite ⚠ (general knowledge; scriptwriters verify) | rounded, lumpy, grey-purple with moss, **never sharp blocks** |
| Surrounding heathland and woods in Drenthe | purple heather `#8E6BA8` and birch trees in the back; heath sheep flocks are a real part of Drenthe heath ⚠ verify before saying so, a visual foreshadow of the lambs |

## Key props to model
1. **Hunebed** (hero): `chunky stylized prehistoric dolmen megalith, big rounded grey granite capstones on thick upright stones, moss on top, soft cartoon storybook, no text`: build as **modular stones** (capstone ×6, uprights ×16) so the kittens can climb / peek under / run between them.
2. **Single boulders** (4 variants, mossy)
3. **Birch and oak trees** (kit), ferns, heather clumps
4. **Info-sign shape** (blank panel, no text) — optional
5. **Glow runes?** no: do **not** invent fake carvings (the real hunebedden have none); if a "magic" glow is needed, use the paw-tag glow on the stones instead.
Safety: kittens **do not climb or stand on** the capstones in shots without a "we are small, the real ones are protected" framing; scriptwriters note site rules (Bible rule 3). Keep poses on the ground/peeking.
