# Ep.5: Kootwijkerzand — "The Dutch Desert"
**Accent:** sand orange `#E8A55B` / heather purple `#9A5FB0` (two-colour accent). **Hero silhouette:** a rolling sand dune ridge with a wind-bent Scots pine.
**Mood:** open, bright, windy, playful; the lambs' first adventure with the cats. Wind is a character: swirls, drifting sand, flying seeds.

## What is true (and what we keep)
| Real element | Cartoon version |
|---|---|
| Largest active drift-sand area in Western Europe, ~700 ha; dunes, heathland and pine forests ([Veluwe.nl](https://veluwe.nl/en/location/het-kootwijkerzand-2/), [Van der Valk Apeldoorn](https://www.vandervalkapeldoorn.nl/en/surroundings/kootwijkerzand-sand-dunes)) | rolling pale-orange dunes with rippled sand texture |
| Irregular, wind-sculpted Scots pines on the sand; wind has blown the roots free ([Komoot guide](https://www.komoot.com/guide/3961088/monuments-naturels-dans-boswachterij-kootwijk)) | **hero pines: twisted, flat-topped, a few with exposed roots** sitting on dune crests |
| Heather blooms purple ([Authentic Chic Lifestyle](https://www.authenticchiclifestyle.com/single-post/purple-heather-hotspots-5-veluwe-must-visits-netherlands)) | patches of purple heather at dune edges: **bloom is late summer (Aug-Sep) ⚠ verify; the episode may be set in bloom** |
| Free access, hiking trails ([Veluwe.nl](https://veluwe.nl/en/location/het-kootwijkerzand-2/)) | one simple sand path with low wooden marker posts |
| "Desert" feel in the middle of the Netherlands: **wind-rippled sand, no sea** | **no camels, no cacti, no pyramids** (don't push a false "Sahara" look); small signs it's Dutch: heather, pines, tidy path posts |
