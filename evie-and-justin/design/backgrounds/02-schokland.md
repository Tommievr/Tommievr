# Ep.2: Schokland — "The Island That Disappeared"
**Accent:** field gold `#E3B93C`. **Hero silhouette:** a low grassy rise with a small church and tiny village, surrounded by flat fields to the horizon.
**Mood:** quiet, nostalgic, mysterious: "this used to be sea". Once an island in the Zuiderzee, now surrounded by farmland (Noordoostpolder reclaimed 1942).

## What is true (and what we keep)
| Real element | Cartoon version |
|---|---|
| Former Zuiderzee island that stopped being an island when the Noordoostpolder was reclaimed in 1942; UNESCO site since 1995 ([Canon Noordoostpolder](https://www.canonnoordoostpolder.nl/en/sea/schokland), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schokland)) | gently raised grassy island in a sea of flat fields |
| People lived on three dwelling mounds: Zuidert, Emmeloord, Middelbuurt ([Boat Bike Tours](https://www.boatbiketours.com/blog/6-reasons-schokland-is-so-special/)) | three small mounds in a line, one hero mound (Middelbuurt) |
| Waterstaatskerk (water-management church) of 1834 amid small wooden houses; houses painted blue and green ([Visit Noordoostpolder / search results](https://www.visitnoordoostpolder.nl/en/locations/2097141634/misthorn-house-schokland), [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/museum-schokland-338)) | **small simple white/cream church**, little timber houses in **blue `#5E8FB5` and green `#6FA37A`** |
| Old shoreline still visible as a raised edge, partly intact retaining wall at Middelbuurt ([CHO](https://culturalheritageonline.com/places/schokland-and-surroundings/)) | **a low stone-and-timber wall along the mound's edge**; stylised "old shoreline" **markers** (see below) |
| Lighthouse keeper's house + foghorn building at the northern mound (Emmeloord); foundation of a lighthouse and church ruins of Ens at the southern tip ([Canon Noordoostpolder](https://www.canonnoordoostpolder.nl/en/sea/schokland), [360cities](https://www.360cities.net/image/lighthouse-ruins-on-the-island-of-schokland)) | small lighthouse with keeper's house on the north end; low ruined-church outline on the south tip |
| Shoreline **poles/markers** the lead mentioned: ⚠ **not confirmed** by my searches. The island's outline is marked in the landscape, details unverified | **design choice:** a dotted line of chunky wooden poles/short stone posts tracing the old shoreline into the fields, with a "wave" colour (blue `#6FB0D8`) painted on the pole tops. **Verify before the script claims this is real.** |
