# Ep.3: Giethoorn — "The Village With No Roads"
**Accent:** canal green `#4FA37A`. **Hero silhouette:** thatched roof + small arched wooden bridge over a narrow canal.
**Mood:** fairytale, intimate, lush: the first location where the kittens are *enclosed* by the set (trees and houses close on both sides).

## What is true (and what we keep)
| Real element | Cartoon version |
|---|---|
| Village of canals, wooden bridges and boat tours instead of roads; thatched-roof cottages on **private islands**; narrow canals instead of streets ([giethoorn.com](https://giethoorn.com/en/), [Swallow's Notes](https://www.swallowsnotes.com/blog/giethoorn-the-village-with-no-roads)) | thatched houses on little islands with a green lawn, a canal, a footbridge to each |
| Canals dug by peat cutters from the 13th century; excavations flooded into the lakes and canals ([Tickets Amsterdam](https://www.tickets-amsterdam.com/giethoorn/about/)) | the canals' edges show **dark peat-coloured banks** `#5A4636` |
| ~170-180 bridges, arched wooden bridges, slim towpaths ([Swallow's Notes](https://www.swallowsnotes.com/blog/giethoorn-the-village-with-no-roads)) | **many small arched wooden bridges**, 3 variants, in repeating rhythm along the canal (≈ 1 bridge per shot as a visual motif) |
| Traditional boats and quiet electric **whisper boats** (open-air, silent motor) + punts that look like gondolas ([Swallow's Notes](https://www.swallowsnotes.com/blog/giethoorn-the-village-with-no-roads), [tangyOcean](https://tangyocean.com/the-ultimate-giethoorn-netherlands-guide-canals-thatched-roofs-dutch-countryside-charm/)) | a flat-bottomed low open boat (whisper boat) in soft colours `#E8DCC8` + `#4FA37A` trim; the kittens can ride it (**safety:** life jackets visible, calm water) |
| "Venice of the North" nickname | optional dialogue only, not a visual |
| Legend of the name (goat horns found by settlers) ⚠ folklore, not verified here | optional: carved goat-horn gable ornament |

## Layered set
| Layer | Content |
|---|---|
| **SKY** | soft, partly hidden by trees; a gap above the canal with warm light |
| **FAR** | rows of poplars and willows, reeds, a distant lake glimmer |
| **MID** | thatched houses (2-3), bridges, boats moored, flower boxes; **canal in the centre as the leading line** |
| **GROUND** | towpath edge/wooden jetty where the characters stand; water at their feet with soft reflections |
| **FG** | **overhanging willow branches and reed stalks**, blurred; lily pads; a duck (small) |

1. **Thatched farmhouse** (hero): `chunky stylized dutch thatched-roof farmhouse, deep thick golden thatch, small windows with shutters, red brick, cartoon storybook, no text`
2. **Arched wooden footbridge** (3 variants) — hero #2
3. **Whisper boat** + **punt** (modular, with and without passengers)
4. Mooring posts, lantern, flower boxes (pink `#E88FB4` and white)
5. Willow tree, reeds, lily pads (reusable kit)
6. Water: a Blender material (glossy, soft ripples, tinted `#3F8A8C`, reflections on)
