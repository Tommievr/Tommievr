## Logline
Four friends walk on the sea floor with a guide and learn that the sea breathes in and out, twice a day.

## Script

**COLD OPEN (0:30)**
[Black screen. Soft, slow waves. Bell: *ting.* Paw tags glow.]
TOFFEE: Where did the water go?
COTTON: It went… out.
[Smash cut: an endless shining mudflat under a soft pink sky. Four small figures on a firm walking path with a guide's shadow ahead.]
EVIE (to camera): Hello, friends! Tonight, we're walking on the sea floor!
JUSTIN: With a guide. Don't try this at home.
[TITLE CARD: Welcome to Evie and Justin's Little Adventures!]

**ARRIVAL & THE QUESTION (1:00)**
[Wide: the mudflats at low tide. Gulls, tiny shining channels, a distant line of sea.]
JUSTIN: I have so many questions. Where is the sea?
COTTON: And will it come back?
TOFFEE: Is it hiding?
EVIE: This is the Wadden Sea. It's a very special place.
GUIDE (off-screen, calm voice): Welcome. Today we walk with the tide.
JUSTIN: A *guide*. Good. We stay close.
EVIE: Because the sea always returns.
[Paw tags glow. Question card: "Where does the sea go?"]

**EXPLORE & DISCOVER (5:00)**
*Scene 1 — Rules of the flats (1:00)*
[The group gathers. Rubber boots, small backpacks on the cats.]
GUIDE: Rule one: always walk with a guide.
JUSTIN: Rule one. Got it.
GUIDE: Rule two: stay together.
COTTON: Together.
GUIDE: Rule three: we watch the tide and the weather.
TOFFEE: Can I run?
GUIDE: Walk. Always walk.
TOFFEE: Walking is… also fun.
EVIE: The sea is gentle, but you must know it.

*Scene 2 — The floor of the sea (1:15)*
[Close-up: paws in soft mud. Small footprints. Shiny channels of water.]
EVIE: Ooh! It's soft and cool.
COTTON: It squishes!
TOFFEE: Squish squish squish!
JUSTIN: Okay, I admit, this is nice.
GUIDE: Look closely. Something lives here.
[Close-up: little curly piles of sand on the surface.]
EVIE: What are those?
GUIDE: Lugworms live under the sand. Those little piles are their leftovers.
TOFFEE: Worm poop!
COTTON: Toffee!
JUSTIN: He's right, though.
GUIDE: Lugworms help keep the sand healthy.

*Scene 3 — Birds, seals and the table (1:15)*
[A distant sandbank with seals resting. Birds probing in the shallows.]
EVIE: Seals! Look, over there.
COTTON: They look so sleepy.
GUIDE: We keep a distance. They need rest.
JUSTIN: Quiet voices, everybody.
[The birds scurry in flocks.]
EVIE: What are they looking for?
GUIDE: Food. When the tide goes out, the mud becomes a table.
TOFFEE: A table with *snacks?*
GUIDE: For many birds, yes.
JUSTIN: So the sea leaves, and dinner appears.

*Scene 4 — The sea comes back (1:30)*
[Mid-shot. Thin sheets of water appear across the flat. Channels widen.]
COTTON: Is that the sea?
GUIDE: It's the tide, coming in. Time to turn back.
JUSTIN: Already?
GUIDE: The tide doesn't ask. It comes in, quietly, but it does come.
[The group walks calmly toward the dike. The water follows in the background.]
TOFFEE: Is it chasing us?
GUIDE: No. It's just doing what it does, twice a day.
EVIE: Twice a day. Out and in.
COTTON: Like breathing.
JUSTIN: A sea that breathes.
[They reach the dike path, dry and safe. Behind them the flats shine, then slowly fill with water.]
EVIE: And here we are, back before it comes.

**CONCEPT EXPLAINED (2:00)**
[Storybook cutaway: the sea, the moon, the earth. A gentle wave arrow.]
EVIE: Justin, do you want to know why the sea goes out and in?
JUSTIN: Always.
EVIE: The moon pulls on the water a little. We call that the tide.
[Cards appear, one at a time, paw-stamped.]
COTTON: One — **Low tide.** The water goes out and the mud shows.
TOFFEE: Two — **High tide.** The water comes back and covers it.
JUSTIN: Three — **Again.** It happens, roughly, twice a day.
EVIE: And all the animals live by that rhythm.
JUSTIN: So the tide is a clock.
COTTON: A slow one.
TOFFEE: A wet one!
EVIE: The sea isn't leaving. It's breathing.
[Gentle music swell.]

**RECAP + TEASER (0:45)**
JUSTIN: Recap! Where does the sea go?
EVIE: Out, and then in again, twice a day…
COTTON: …pulled by the moon…
TOFFEE: …and the mud turns into a table for birds!
JUSTIN: And we always walk with a guide.
EVIE: This is the end of our first big season, friends.
COTTON: Will we see you again?
JUSTIN: We'll meet again. (smiles) There's a lot of Netherlands left.
[Teaser card: "More little adventures soon."]

**GOODNIGHT OUTRO (0:30)**
[Dusk on the dike. The water returns slowly. Music slows. Four small figures sit close together.]
COTTON: It's so peaceful.
TOFFEE (yawning): I can hear the sea.
EVIE: It's saying goodnight.
JUSTIN: Goodnight, sea. Goodnight, mud. Goodnight, worms.
COTTON: Goodnight.
TOFFEE: Gnight.
[Paw tags glow, then dim. Bell *ting…* Fade out.]

## Shot notes / visual ideas
- Mudflat as a huge mirror: sky ~60% of every wide. Pink/gold sky, silver-blue water channels.
- The guide is never shown in full: shadow, boots, calm voice, a hand holding a staff. No new character on screen (see questions in lead/inbox/scripts.md).
- Cats wear small boots and tiny packs in the mud; Toffee never runs off; no wandering alone.
- Seals and birds shown at a respectful distance. Quiet voices.
- Cards and cutaway diagram: moon + earth + water as simple icons; no real-physics text.
- Closing: Justin hints at "more adventures soon" to leave the series open.
- Low-compute friendly: stills + parallax + mouth swap.
