## Logline
Two kittens follow a bell into a desert in the Netherlands, meet two lambs, and learn how wind shapes a landscape that never sits still.

## Script

**COLD OPEN (0:30)**
[Black screen. Wind. *Ting… ting…* A paw tag glows.]
JUSTIN (whisper): Evie. It's the bell again.
EVIE: And it's coming from…
[Smash cut: rolling pale sand dunes under a huge blue sky. Two small silhouettes bounce along a ridge.]
EVIE (to camera): Hello, friends! We followed the bell into a desert!
JUSTIN: A desert. In the Netherlands. Don't ask me how.
EVIE: We'll ask!
JUSTIN: Welcome to Evie and Justin's Little Adventures!
[TITLE CARD]

**ARRIVAL & THE QUESTION (1:00)**
[The kittens climb a small dune. At the top, two lambs: one cream with blue eyes, hiding half behind the other; one brown with green eyes, bouncing.]
TOFFEE: Hi! Hi hi hi! You followed our bells!
COTTON (softly): Toffee. Slowly.
TOFFEE: I'm doing slowly!
EVIE: Hello. I'm Evie.
JUSTIN: I'm Justin. Are you the bell?
COTTON: I'm Cotton. This is Toffee. We live nearby.
TOFFEE: We heard you were looking at old stones. We *love* stones.
JUSTIN: Okay. Now, I have a question. A desert. Here. Why?
COTTON: This is the Kootwijkerzand.
EVIE: Kootwijkerzand… sand that moves!
[Paw tags glow. Question card: "Why is there a desert here?"]

**EXPLORE & DISCOVER (5:00)**
*Scene 1 — Long ago, a forest (1:15)*
[Cotton leads them along the marked path. Flashback illustration: green forest.]
COTTON: Long ago, trees grew here.
JUSTIN: Trees? Where are they?
COTTON: In the Middle Ages, people cut down a lot of forest. They needed wood for fires.
TOFFEE: Chop chop chop!
COTTON: Without trees and plants, the soil was bare. Then the wind could pick it up.
EVIE: So the forest wasn't *lost*. It was… moved. Piece by piece.
[Flashback: green trees turning to pale sand, grain by grain.]

*Scene 2 — Wind and sand (1:30)*
[They watch the wind lift tiny grains. Close-up of sand grains hopping.]
JUSTIN: Okay, but how does wind move sand? Sand's heavy.
COTTON: It hops. A little gust picks up a grain, it jumps forward, and drops down.
TOFFEE: Bounce! Bounce! Bounce!
COTTON: And when it lands, it knocks other grains loose.
JUSTIN: A hopping chain reaction.
EVIE: Like a very, very small avalanche.
COTTON: A bigger wind, and the whole dune slowly shifts.
[Animation: a dune crest moving like a slow wave across a few seconds.]
JUSTIN: So the whole landscape walks.
COTTON: Very slowly. But it does.

*Scene 3 — Toffee's big jump (1:00)*
[Toffee spots a steep slope.]
TOFFEE: I'm going to jump down that!
COTTON: Toffee, no! The tiny plants are growing there.
TOFFEE: Oh. Oh no. Sorry, plants!
[Toffee skids and tumbles sideways, landing on the path. Dust puff.]
EVIE: Are you okay?
TOFFEE: (muffled, from the sand) I'm sandy.
JUSTIN: Stay on the path, everybody. Tiny plants need us to be careful.
COTTON: This place is a nature reserve. Staying on the marked path keeps it safe.

*Scene 4 — Life in the desert (1:15)*
[Close-ups: small plants, lichens, a beetle, a small bird.]
EVIE: Look at how much is growing here!
COTTON: Special plants live in the sand. They like places that are open, dry and sunny.
JUSTIN: But how do they stay put if the sand moves?
COTTON: Some stay low and tough, holding the sand with their roots.
EVIE: So the plants are like tiny anchors.
COTTON: And the little animals live around them.
JUSTIN: So even a desert is a home.
COTTON: It's a different kind of home.

**CONCEPT EXPLAINED (2:00)**
[Storybook cutaway: a dune slice, wind arrows, roots, little animals.]
EVIE: Okay, friends. Let's put it together. What makes a drift sand?
[Cards appear, one at a time, paw-stamped.]
JUSTIN: One — **Bare sand.** The plants and trees are gone.
COTTON: Two — **Wind.** It makes the grains hop and the dunes move.
TOFFEE: Three — **Tiny plants.** They hold on, and bring life back!
EVIE: And it all depends on wind, sand and time.
JUSTIN: And a bit of *Justin* knowing what a dune is.
TOFFEE: And a bit of me falling in it!
EVIE: A drift sand isn't broken. It's *alive*, and it keeps changing.
COTTON: In nature, "changing" isn't a problem. It's how it lives.
[Gentle music swell.]

**RECAP + TEASER (0:45)**
JUSTIN: Recap! Why is there a desert in the Netherlands?
EVIE: Trees were cut down long ago…
COTTON: …the wind moved the bare sand…
TOFFEE: …and tiny plants grow back on it!
JUSTIN: Cotton. Toffee. Do you want to see something *huge* next?
TOFFEE: How huge?
JUSTIN: A giant gate, against the sea.
COTTON: (gasps) The sea?
EVIE: We'll show you.
[Teaser card: "Next: The Gates Against the Sea".]

**GOODNIGHT OUTRO (0:30)**
[Golden hour. Four small shapes on a dune. Music slows. Cotton's bell rings softly.]
COTTON: I've never had friends from far away before.
EVIE: Now you do.
TOFFEE (yawning): Can I sleep on this dune?
JUSTIN: Not on that one. Let's find the cosy one.
EVIE: Goodnight, friends.
COTTON: Goodnight.
TOFFEE: Gnight! (tiny snore)
[Paw tags glow, then dim. Bell *ting…* Fade out.]

## Shot notes / visual ideas
- Golden-hour sand, pale and warm; huge sky (~50% of every wide).
- Introduce lambs against the dune crest as silhouettes first, then reveal: Cotton cream/blue eyes/light-blue collar; Toffee brown/green eyes/green collar. Keep eye and collar colours consistent.
- Cotton's bell = the same *ting* sound planted since Ep.2.
- Four-voice scenes: keep lines to one short sentence each; use each character's catchphrase tone (Toffee excited, Cotton gentle, Justin dry, Evie soft).
- Sand grain animation: simple dots hopping; big dune motion as a slow loop.
- Respect: cats and lambs stay on marked paths; Toffee's off-path slide is shown as a mistake and corrected right away (no behaviour to copy).
- Low-compute friendly: stills + parallax + mouth swap.
