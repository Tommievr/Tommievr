## Logline
Two kittens follow a bell into a desert in the Netherlands, meet two lambs, and learn how wind shapes a landscape that never sits still.

## Script

**COLD OPEN (0:30)**
[Black screen. Wind. *Ting… ting…* A paw tag glows.]
JUSTIN (whisper): Evie. It's the bell again.
EVIE: Ooh. I hear it too.
JUSTIN: It's following us. Or we're following it. I can't tell anymore.
EVIE: And it's coming from…
[Smash cut: rolling pale sand dunes under a huge blue sky. Two small silhouettes bounce along a ridge.]
EVIE (to camera): Hello, friends! We followed the bell into a desert!
JUSTIN: A desert. In the Netherlands. Don't ask me how.
EVIE: We'll ask!
JUSTIN: Wait, that was a joke. I meant we will ask. Obviously.
JUSTIN: Welcome to Evie and Justin's Little Adventures!
[TITLE CARD]

**ARRIVAL & THE QUESTION (1:00)**
[The kittens climb a small dune. At the top, two lambs: one cream with blue eyes, hiding half behind the other; one brown with green eyes, bouncing.]
TOFFEE: Hi! Hi hi hi! You followed our bells!
COTTON (softly): Toffee. Slowly.
TOFFEE: I'm doing slowly!
TOFFEE: This is my slowly face.
EVIE: Hello. I'm Evie.
JUSTIN: I'm Justin. Are you the bell?
COTTON: I'm Cotton. This is Toffee.
COTTON: Sorry. I'm a bit shy with new friends.
EVIE: That's okay. I'm happy you rang.
COTTON (smiling): Really? Then I'll ring more.
EVIE: Your bell is so pretty. Is that a heart?
COTTON: Yes. It rings when I'm happy.
TOFFEE: Mine rings when I trip.
JUSTIN: Then yours never stops.
TOFFEE: Ha! True!
[Cotton's bell goes *ting*. Evie's tag glows softly.]
TOFFEE: I'm Toffee! I jump! I run! I fall over a lot!
JUSTIN: Good to know. Very useful.
COTTON: We live nearby. We know every bump of this place.
TOFFEE: We heard you were looking at old stones. We *love* stones.
JUSTIN: Okay. Now, I have a question. A desert. Here. Why?
JUSTIN: Deserts have camels. And cacti. Where are the camels?
TOFFEE: I can be a camel!
JUSTIN: You have no humps, Toffee.
TOFFEE: I have a fluffy bump.
COTTON: This is the Kootwijkerzand.
EVIE: Kootwijkerzand… sand that moves!
[Paw tags glow. Question card: "Why is there a desert here?"]

**EXPLORE & DISCOVER (5:00)**
*Scene 1 — Long ago, a forest (1:15)*
[Cotton leads them along the marked path. Flashback illustration: green forest.]
COTTON: Long ago, trees grew here.
JUSTIN: Trees? Where are they?
COTTON: In the Middle Ages, people cut down a lot of forest.
TOFFEE: Chop chop chop!
COTTON: They needed wood for fires.
EVIE: Poor trees.
COTTON: Without trees and plants, the soil was bare.
JUSTIN: And bare is bad?
COTTON: Bare means nothing holds the sand down. Then the wind can pick it up.
EVIE: So the forest wasn't *lost*. It was… moved. Piece by piece.
TOFFEE: Moved where?
COTTON: Around the dunes, grain by grain.
JUSTIN: So the desert is a forest that went on holiday.
TOFFEE: Without telling anyone!
EVIE: I wonder what the forest felt like, long ago.
COTTON: Quiet. Shady. Full of birdsong, maybe.
EVIE: And now the sky is so big.
COTTON: Big sky, big sand. Different, but still beautiful.
[Flashback: green trees turning to pale sand, grain by grain.]

*Scene 2 — Wind and sand (1:30)*
[They watch the wind lift tiny grains. Close-up of sand grains hopping.]
JUSTIN: Okay, but how does wind move sand? Sand's heavy.
COTTON: It hops. A little gust picks up a grain.
EVIE: And then?
COTTON: It jumps forward, and drops down.
TOFFEE: Bounce! Bounce! Bounce!
JUSTIN: That's you, Toffee.
TOFFEE: I'm a sand grain! Whee!
COTTON: And when a grain lands, it knocks other grains loose.
JUSTIN: A hopping chain reaction.
EVIE: Like a very, very small avalanche.
JUSTIN: Or dominoes. Except the dominoes jump.
TOFFEE: Can I be a domino?
JUSTIN: Please don't fall on anyone.
TOFFEE: Too late! Oops. Sorry, Justin.
COTTON: Hop, land, knock. Hop, land, knock.
EVIE: Ooh. It's like the sand is dancing.
COTTON: A bigger wind, and the whole dune slowly shifts.
[Animation: a dune crest moving like a slow wave across a few seconds.]
JUSTIN: So the whole landscape walks.
COTTON: Very slowly. But it does.
TOFFEE: Can it walk to my house?
JUSTIN: Let's hope it takes the path.
EVIE: Do dunes ever get tired?
COTTON: They just keep going, whenever the wind blows.
JUSTIN: So they're never late. They're just slow.
EVIE: I like a landscape that never sits still.
JUSTIN: Then it's lucky you're a kitten. You never sit still either.

*Scene 3 — Toffee's big jump (1:00)*
[Toffee spots a steep slope.]
TOFFEE: I'm going to jump down that!
COTTON: Toffee, no! The tiny plants are growing there.
TOFFEE: Oh. Oh no. Sorry, plants!
[Toffee skids and tumbles sideways, landing on the path. Dust puff.]
EVIE: Are you okay?
TOFFEE: (muffled, from the sand) I'm sandy.
JUSTIN: You're very sandy.
TOFFEE: I'm a sand lamb now.
COTTON: Are the plants okay?
EVIE: I think they're fine. Nobody landed on them.
TOFFEE: Phew. Sorry again, plants.
JUSTIN: Stay on the path, everybody. Tiny plants need us to be careful.
COTTON: This place is a nature reserve. Staying on the marked path keeps it safe.
EVIE: Little plants have little homes. We should be gentle.
JUSTIN: Big paws, small plants. Careful steps.
TOFFEE: Gentle. I can do gentle. Watch.
[Toffee tiptoes very, very slowly along the path. Nobody blinks. Bell: *ting*.]
JUSTIN: That's the slowest I've ever seen you.
TOFFEE: I'm doing gentle!

*Scene 4 — Life in the desert (1:15)*
[Close-ups: small plants, lichens, a beetle, a small bird.]
EVIE: Look at how much is growing here!
JUSTIN: In a desert?
COTTON: Special plants live in the sand. They like places that are open, dry and sunny.
TOFFEE: Like a beach day!
JUSTIN: But how do they stay put if the sand moves?
COTTON: Some stay low and tough, holding the sand with their roots.
EVIE: So the plants are like tiny anchors.
JUSTIN: Anchors on a sandy sea.
COTTON: Yes. Exactly like that.
JUSTIN: And the roots? Like fingers holding on?
COTTON: A bit like that, yes.
TOFFEE: So plants are sand huggers!
EVIE: Sand huggers. I love that.
JUSTIN: Tiny, tough sand huggers.
EVIE: Ooh, look! Something's moving!
[Close-up: a small beetle, then a small bird, crossing the sand.]
COTTON: The little animals live around the plants.
TOFFEE: Hello, little beetle! I'm not jumping, I promise!
EVIE: Shh. Don't scare the little bird.
JUSTIN: So even a desert is a home.
COTTON: It's a different kind of home.
EVIE: A home that moves a little every day.
COTTON: And everyone living here is used to it.
JUSTIN: Moving house without packing. Smart.
TOFFEE: I can never find my socks when I move.
JUSTIN: You don't wear socks.
TOFFEE: That's why!

**CONCEPT EXPLAINED (2:00)**
[Storybook cutaway: a dune slice, wind arrows, roots, little animals.]
EVIE: Okay, friends. Let's put it together. What makes a drift sand?
JUSTIN: Three ingredients. Like a recipe.
EVIE: Wind, sand and plants. Easy to remember.
TOFFEE: Is there cake?
JUSTIN: No cake. Sorry.
TOFFEE: Worst recipe ever.
[Cards appear, one at a time, paw-stamped.]
JUSTIN: One — **Bare sand.** The plants and trees are gone.
EVIE: With nothing to hold it, the sand is free to go.
COTTON: Two — **Wind.** It makes the grains hop and the dunes move.
JUSTIN: Hop, land, knock. Got it.
TOFFEE: Three — **Tiny plants.** They hold on, and bring life back!
COTTON: Little by little.
JUSTIN: So a plant is a slow, patient hero.
TOFFEE: I'm a fast, bouncy hero.
COTTON: You're a hero who nearly jumped on the plants.
EVIE: Like a tug of war. Wind pulls, roots hold.
JUSTIN: And nobody wins forever.
EVIE: And it all depends on wind, sand and time.
JUSTIN: And a bit of *Justin* knowing what a dune is.
TOFFEE: And a bit of me falling in it!
COTTON: And a bit of friends who show you around.
EVIE: Aww, Cotton.
EVIE: A drift sand isn't broken. It's *alive*, and it keeps changing.
JUSTIN: Like a puzzle that keeps rearranging itself.
COTTON: In nature, "changing" isn't a problem. It's how it lives.
EVIE: Ooh. That's a nice thing to remember.
COTTON: Even when it feels shaky, it can still be home.
EVIE: Especially then. Home is who you share it with.
JUSTIN: Even when your sandwich blows away.
TOFFEE: Wait, whose sandwich?
[Gentle music swell.]

[Golden hour. Four small shapes on a dune. Music slows. Cotton's bell rings softly.]
COTTON: I've never had friends from far away before.
EVIE: Now you do.
COTTON: Will you come back?
JUSTIN: Of course. Who else will ask all the questions?
TOFFEE (yawning): Can I sleep on this dune?
JUSTIN: Not on that one. It might walk off.
EVIE: Let's find the cosy one.

## Shot notes / visual ideas
- Golden-hour sand, pale and warm; huge sky (~50% of every wide).
- Introduce lambs against the dune crest as silhouettes first, then reveal: Cotton cream/blue eyes/light-blue collar; Toffee brown/green eyes/green collar. Keep eye and collar colours consistent.
- Cotton's bell = the same *ting* sound planted since Ep.2.
- Four-voice scenes: keep lines to one short sentence each; use each character's catchphrase tone (Toffee excited, Cotton gentle, Justin dry, Evie soft).
- Sand grain animation: simple dots hopping; big dune motion as a slow loop.
- Respect: cats and lambs stay on marked paths; Toffee's off-path slide is shown as a mistake and corrected right away (no behaviour to copy).
- Low-compute friendly: stills + parallax + mouth swap.
