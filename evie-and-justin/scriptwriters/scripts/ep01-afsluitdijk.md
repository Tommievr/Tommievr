## Logline
Two kittens ask how a country can sit below the sea, and on the long dike that answers them they find a little bottle of bedtime magic that lets them stay little and keep exploring.

## Script

**COLD OPEN (0:30)**
[Black screen. Soft wind. A tiny gold paw-tag glows.]
JUSTIN (whisper): Evie. Are we… *lower* than the sea right now?
EVIE: (gasp) Justin, look!
[Smash cut: a huge sky, a never-ending road, water on BOTH sides. The kittens hop out onto the dike.]
EVIE (to camera): Hello, friends! Tonight's bedtime adventure starts… early!
JUSTIN: Welcome to Evie and Justin's Little Adventures!
[TITLE CARD]

**ARRIVAL & THE QUESTION (1:00)**
[Wide: the Afsluitdijk stretching to the horizon, sea left, calm lake right.]
EVIE: Ooh, it's like a road in the middle of the water.
JUSTIN: Water on that side. Water on this side. Evie, which one is the sea?
EVIE: The rough side is the Wadden Sea. The calm side is a lake called the IJsselmeer.
JUSTIN: Wait. The sea is right *there* and the Netherlands is right *there*. Why doesn't the sea just… walk in?
EVIE: That's a good question for a kitten who was supposed to be asleep.
JUSTIN: *We* were supposed to be asleep. In our storybook. And then our paw tags started glowing.
EVIE: And here we are.
[Paw tags glow. Question card: "How can a country live below the sea?"]
EVIE: That's tonight's mystery!

**EXPLORE & DISCOVER (5:00)**
*Scene 1 — The long walk (1:00)*
[The kittens trot along. Wind ruffles fur. A gull zooms past; Justin jumps.]
JUSTIN: I wasn't scared. I was… stretching.
EVIE: This dike is about thirty-two kilometres long!
JUSTIN: Thirty-two? My paws are tiny, Evie. That's, like, a hundred thousand Justin-steps.
EVIE: Longer. We'd be here till bedtime.
JUSTIN: Which is when we're supposed to be asleep. Keep walking.
EVIE: Look how the sky goes on forever.
JUSTIN: Fine. The sky is very good.
EVIE: I wonder what the sea thinks about all of this.
JUSTIN: The sea thinks: "Why is there a wall in my way?"
EVIE: Or maybe: "What a lovely path for two small cats."
JUSTIN: You always give the sea the nicest voice.
EVIE: Somebody has to!

*Scene 2 — The monument (1:00)*
[They reach a tall monument with a statue of a man.]
EVIE: Someone important is up there.
JUSTIN: It's an engineer! Cornelis Lely. He had a big idea: close off the old sea bay and make the land safer.
EVIE: Because long ago a storm could sweep right in.
[Illustrated flashback, storybook style: waves rushing toward cosy houses; people looking worried.]
JUSTIN: So people built a very, very long wall. A dike!
[Flashback: workers, boats, stones, clay.]
EVIE: And in 1932 they closed the very last gap.
JUSTIN: Imagine that day. A whole bay, closed.
EVIE: I can almost hear it. Everyone cheering. Boats blowing their horns.
JUSTIN: And someone saying, "Please, let's all go home and have something warm to drink."
EVIE: (laughs) Very Dutch.
[Illustrated moment: tiny storybook people waving hats, a little boat tooting.]

*Scene 3 — The salty lake that changed (1:00)*
[Evie dips a paw into the calm side.]
EVIE: Justin… this water isn't salty.
JUSTIN: (tastes, makes a face) Blegh! You're right.
EVIE: It used to be salty sea. Once the dike shut it in, fresh water kept filling it. Now it's a lake.
JUSTIN: A whole sea turned into a lake. By a wall. That's the most dramatic glow-up ever.
EVIE: The water changed its whole story, just because it moved to a new side of the wall.
JUSTIN: Water has a very long bedtime story.
EVIE: (smiling) And speaking of glowing…
[The paw tags glow brighter. A soft silver light appears at the foot of the monument.]

*Scene 4 — The little bottle (1:15)*
[A tiny glass bottle rests in the grass, glowing like moonlight. A little paper label hangs from its neck.]
EVIE: Justin. It's a bottle. And it's glowing just like our tags.
JUSTIN: Okay, that's suspicious. Bottles don't glow. Not even in this country.
[Evie reads the label, slowly, with a wondering voice.]
EVIE: "Moonmilk. For kind and curious explorers. Two sips: stay as you are, and keep exploring."
JUSTIN: Stay as we are?
EVIE: Little. And curious. For a very long time.
JUSTIN: Hold on. Rule one of being an explorer: you NEVER drink something you find. Never ever.
EVIE: That's very true, Justin. And real friends must always ask a grown-up first. Always.
JUSTIN: This is a *story*. It's bedtime-story magic. In real life, we don't taste mystery bottles.
EVIE: Right. But we're storybook kittens. And I think… this bottle was left for us.
[Beat. The two kittens look at each other. The tags pulse in time, like two small heartbeats.]
JUSTIN: One sip each. Together.
EVIE: Together.
[They sip. Silver sparkles rise around them, then settle on their fur like tiny stars. The bottle fades to empty glass; a little light floats up and away.]
JUSTIN (softly): I feel… exactly the same.
EVIE (smiling): Just a bit sparkly.
JUSTIN: Tastes like warm milk and a lullaby.
EVIE: So now we can keep exploring. As long as we like.
JUSTIN: That's a *lot* of Netherlands.
EVIE: Windmills, islands, giant stones, the sea floor…
JUSTIN: Wait, the sea floor? We can walk on that?
EVIE: Maybe. One adventure at a time.
JUSTIN: One adventure at a time. Our tags can keep the list.
[Gentle music swell.]

*Scene 5 — The gates (0:45)*
[They walk toward the big sluice gates at the end.]
JUSTIN: Okay. Back to the real mystery. If the lake keeps filling up, where does the water go?
EVIE: Out through the gates! When the sea outside is low, the gates open and let the extra water out. When the sea is high, they close.
JUSTIN: And boats?
EVIE: Ships pass through special locks.
JUSTIN: So it's a wall with doors. Smart doors.
EVIE: Doors that listen to the tides.
JUSTIN: Doors that open when the sea is polite, and close when the sea is grumpy.
EVIE: Exactly. And people decide when.
JUSTIN: So it's not magic. It's careful, clever people.
EVIE: (grinning) Plus one very small bit of magic back there.
JUSTIN: Which we are not telling the dike about.

**CONCEPT EXPLAINED (2:00)**
[Cut to storybook-style cutaway diagram: sea, dike, land, cats as markers.]
EVIE: So here's the secret. A lot of the Netherlands is very low. Some of it is even below sea level!
JUSTIN: Which sounds like a terrible idea.
EVIE: But the Dutch learned: don't fight the water — manage it. Three steps:
[Three cards appear, one by one, each with a paw-stamp.]
EVIE: One — **Keep it out.** Dikes and dunes make walls.
JUSTIN: Two — **Move it away.** Gates, canals and pumps send extra water where it should go.
EVIE: Three — **Keep watching.** People check the water and weather every day.
JUSTIN: So the land is dry because people take care of it. Every day.
EVIE: A little like tucking in a blanket.
JUSTIN: A *very* big blanket.
EVIE: And it doesn't tuck itself in. People check it, mend it, and make it stronger when it needs help.
JUSTIN: Like a good bedtime routine. Brush teeth, check dike, go to sleep.
EVIE: (giggles) In that order!
EVIE: And people have been tucking it in for a long, long time.
JUSTIN: Which means we get to keep visiting. See how it changes.
EVIE: Ooh. Like a story that keeps going.
JUSTIN: The dike keeps the water out so people can keep their stories going. And we get to keep telling ours.
EVIE: That's the best part. Keeping things safe so they can last.
JUSTIN: Okay, now you're making *me* sentimental.
[Gentle music swell.]

**RECAP + TEASER (0:45)**
JUSTIN: Okay, quick recap. What did we learn?
EVIE: Dikes keep the sea out…
JUSTIN: …gates let water out…
EVIE: …and the Dutch keep watching and caring for it together!
JUSTIN: And bottles that glow are for *stories*, not for real life.
EVIE: Never taste a mystery. Ask a grown-up first!
JUSTIN: Not even a tiny one.
EVIE: Not even a tiny one. Not even a glowing one.
JUSTIN: Next time, Evie, I want to find a place that *used to be an island.*
EVIE: Ooh! An island that's now in the middle of the fields?
JUSTIN: (grinning) Maybe.
[Teaser card: "Next: The Island That Disappeared".]

**GOODNIGHT OUTRO (0:30)**
[Sun low and golden. Music slows. The kittens sit together at the dike's edge, tiny silver sparkles still in their fur.]
EVIE (yawning): That was a big adventure for little cats.
JUSTIN: And we'll *stay* little cats. With a lot more adventures.
EVIE: A new place every time. A new question every time.
JUSTIN: And I'll ask the questions. You'll do the wondering.
EVIE: That sounds just right.
EVIE: Even the biggest wall started with one little stone.
JUSTIN: And the best stories start with one little bottle.
EVIE: Goodnight, friends.
JUSTIN: Goodnight. And goodnight, sea. Stay on your side.
[Paw tags glow, then dim. Fade out.]

## Shot notes / visual ideas
- Keep the dike as a strong horizontal line; cats small in frame for scale.
- Sea side = silvery-teal, lake side = calmer, brighter. Sky takes ~50% of every wide shot.
- Flashbacks as flat storybook illustration (cheap to make, clearly different).
- **Moonmilk bottle (canon proposal):** tiny rounded glass bottle, pearl-white glow like moonlight, cork shaped like a paw print, paper label tied with a thin blue ribbon. The paw tags glow in the same silver-white when it is near. After drinking: soft silver sparkles on the fur for a moment, nothing else changes. No transformation, no dark mood.
- Moonmilk beat is wondrous and warm, not mysterious-scary: golden hour, gentle music, no jump cuts.
- Kid-safety beat is spoken by Justin first (rule), then Evie (ask a grown-up), then repeated lightly in the recap. Keep both in the final cut.
- Do not show the cats climbing on the monument; the bottle rests in the grass at its foot.
- Concept cards: simple icons, large paw-stamp animation, no text-heavy slides.
- Low-compute friendly: most scenes are stills + parallax + mouth swap.
