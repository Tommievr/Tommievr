Funding is estimated from the Kraken hourly funding rates since each entry (+ received, − paid); Rate is the current hourly funding rate (green when it pays you, red when you pay). ≈ Liq is the bot's own liquidation estimate per coin; on the shared cross-margin account the real level depends on all positions together. Fees are the trading fees paid since the 7 Oct restart (0.05% taker, 0.02% maker), estimated from the bot's fills; the balance is the real Kraken wallet, so it is already after fees and funding. P&L % is the price move since entry; leverage multiplies it on the account.
