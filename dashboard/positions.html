Prices are live Kraken prices, read by this page every minute (spot, within a few hundredths of a % of the futures mark). Positions, funding and fees refresh every few hours; the account value is estimated from the last read plus the price moves since. Funding is estimated from Kraken's hourly funding rates since each entry (+ received, − paid); Rate is the current hourly funding rate (green when it pays the position, red when it costs). Fees are the estimated trading fees of the fills that opened each position (0.05% taker, 0.02% maker); the account value is already after fees and funding. ≈ Liq is a rough liquidation price if only that coin moved; with cross margin the real level depends on all positions together. P&L % is the price move since entry; leverage multiplies it on the account.
